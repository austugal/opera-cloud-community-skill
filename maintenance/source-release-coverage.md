## Matrix interpretation and gaps

- “No file-level source review date recorded” means the reference cites a source but does not state
  when the relevant passage was read. It is not a claim that the source is stale or current.
- The 2026-10-15 date is the matrix inventory date. It must not be substituted for a source review
  date unless the reference explicitly records that check.
- The table identifies cited primary entry points and documented evidence boundaries. It does not
  show that every URL was reachable during this inventory, or that the linked page is unchanged.
- Release and country coverage is selective. There is no exhaustive release-by-release, country-by-
  country, tenant, entitlement, certification, or API operation coverage. The rotating research
  backlog remains open.
- No primary external source was fetched or uploaded while preparing this inventory. Verify the
  deployed product/release and current Oracle/authority/vendor material before relying on a
  release-sensitive, operational, integration, tax, or legal assertion.
