A small interactive experiment

Guide a robot through a maze.

This original little robot follows your page scroll along a winding maze. Scroll down to lead it forward, or scroll up to retrace your path. It only moves in response to your scrolling—there is no autoplay.

Scroll to get started

01 / Turn

Take the first corner.

The robot's position is tied to the document's scroll progress. It stays beside the reading area, tracing the route as you move through this page.

02 / Explore

Every turn is yours.

There is no timer or score to chase. The maze is a visual path, and your scroll direction decides which way the robot travels.

03 / Pause

Stop whenever you like.

Use the pause control in the corner to stop following scroll. Resume when you're ready; the robot continues from its current place.

04 / Finish

Backtracking works too.

Scroll back up and the robot retraces the maze in reverse. The motion is deliberately simple and stays under your control.
